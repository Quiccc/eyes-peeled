Eyes Peeled Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Eyes Peeled is a daily photo scavenger-hunt game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app and its server collect, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. "We" means the Eyes Peeled developer. Questions go to privacy@eyespeeled.app.

What we collect

  • Account details from Sign in with Apple or Google. We receive an account identifier from Apple or Google that only identifies you to us, and your name if you share it. We do not store your email address or password.
  • Profile. The username you choose, and a profile picture if you add one (taken with your camera or chosen from your photos; we remove its location and other metadata before uploading).
  • Photos you submit. When you photograph a daily word, the app shrinks and re-encodes the photo on your phone, which removes its metadata, including GPS location, before it is uploaded. We store the photo, a fingerprint of it (used to catch the same photo being reused), the time it was received, and the result of the check.
  • Game activity. Your points, ranks, streaks and finds.
  • Social activity. Friend requests, friends, people you block, and reports you send about other players or photos.
  • Purchases. If you buy Premium, whether you have it. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • Advertising ID. If you are not Premium, the app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. On iOS this is only used with your permission.
  • Technical data. Our server keeps short-lived logs (such as IP address, time and the request made) to run the service and keep it secure.

The app uses your camera only when you take a photo for the game. It does not access your contacts, location or photo library.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To check whether your photo shows the daily word, score it, and run the leaderboards, streaks and friend feed.
  • To moderate the game: review reported photos, remove inappropriate ones and block abusive accounts.
  • To provide Premium and to show ads to players who are not Premium.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see what

  • All players can see your username, name, profile picture, points and rank on leaderboards and profiles, and can find you by username.
  • Your friends can also see the photos you found today. Players who are not your friends cannot see your photos.
  • People you block cannot find you or see you, and you cannot see them.

Who we share it with

We share only what each service needs to do its job for us:

  • Anthropic (Claude): each submitted photo and the daily word are sent to Anthropic's API to decide whether the word appears in the photo. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train its models.
  • Hostinger: hosts our server, database, photos and backups.
  • Apple and Google: provide sign-in and handle in-app purchases.
  • RevenueCat: tracks Premium purchases, linked to your Eyes Peeled account identifier.
  • Google AdMob: shows ads and receives your advertising ID and basic device information. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, photos and game history while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is erased from our live systems straight away. Copies in our nightly backups are deleted automatically within 14 days. Reports you sent about others are kept for moderation, but no longer linked to you.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the Profile tab and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, finds, friends, blocks and sign-in sessions. It also asks RevenueCat to delete your purchase record and, for Apple accounts, revokes Eyes Peeled's access to your Apple ID. If you can no longer open the app, email privacy@eyespeeled.app from the account you want deleted and we will delete it for you.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. On iOS, you can refuse tracking when asked.
  • Withdraw camera access at any time in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how we use it. Email us to do so, and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Photos are served only through short-lived signed links. Sign-in sessions use tokens that we store only in hashed form. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Children

Eyes Peeled is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
